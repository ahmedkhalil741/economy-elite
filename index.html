What we do

Four ways to ride with us

Airport transportation

Newark, LaGuardia, JFK and Teterboro. We watch your flight, so a delay never costs you the car — or an extra fee.

Local & point-to-point

Dinner, a doctor's appointment, the train station, a school run. Short trips around New Providence and Union County, one flat price.

NYC transportation

Manhattan and the boroughs, in and out, by reservation. A fixed fare agreed before you get in.

Long-distance & special trips

Philadelphia, the shore, a wedding, a hospital transfer, a night out with stops along the way. Tell us the plan and we'll quote it.

Over time

Ride with us, and it counts

The more we drive you, the better we know you. We remember how you like to travel, recognize your loyalty, and make every ride feel a little more familiar.

There is nothing to sign up for. Your phone number is the account. Book once and we remember how you like to travel — the cabin warm or cool, a car seat ready, a hand to the door if you need one. You never have to explain it twice.

That is the part that matters, and it starts on your second ride. The credits below are how we say so out loud. Every completed ride earns a point, and points turn into credit off a future trip.

We'll text you when you've earned something. No app, no card in your wallet, no email list.

Always We remember you Your cabin temperature, the car seat, the Suburban or the sedan, a text before we arrive. From the second ride on, it's already set.
$5 After your first ride A thank-you for trying us, off your next trip.
$15 At ten rides You become a Loyal Customer for good — and we mark the milestone with $15 toward your next ride.
$10 Every ten rides after that Twenty, thirty, forty. It keeps going.
$10 When you refer someone Credited once they've taken their first ride. Tell us who sent them when you book.
$10 On your birthday Tell us the day and we'll remember. We don't need the year.
Never Your points expire They only ever go up, and spending your credit never costs you your standing.

Credit comes off a future ride and isn't paid out in cash. Points are counted when a ride is completed — a cancelled trip doesn't count, and doesn't cost you one either.

35 minutes of complimentary waiting on domestic arrivals, 45 on international. If your flight runs late, we cover the extra wait.
