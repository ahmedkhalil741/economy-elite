What we do

Four ways to ride with us

Airport transportation

Newark, LaGuardia, JFK and Teterboro. We watch your flight, so a delay never costs you the car — or an extra fee.

Local & point-to-point

Dinner, a doctor's appointment, the train station, a school run. Short trips around New Providence and Union County, one flat price.

NYC transportation

Manhattan and the boroughs, in and out, by reservation. A fixed fare agreed before you get in.

Long-distance & special trips

Philadelphia, the shore, a wedding, a hospital transfer, a night out with stops along the way. Tell us the plan and we'll quote it.
